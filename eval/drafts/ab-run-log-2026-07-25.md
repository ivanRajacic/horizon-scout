### hyb-08 - textiles x ERC-COG - ACCEPTED (2 passes, 1 fix, candidate 1 of 3)

**Round 1.** Drafter returned a filter-read question asking what INTERACT's electrospun
liquid-crystal fibres are meant to *detect*. `precheck_record` ok on first call;
`validate-record` OK. The drafter recorded its own doubt unprompted: the gold came back at
unscoped rank 1 in all four conditions.

Critic (fresh) reported two findings:

- **FILTER-DECORATION, HIGH.** Two independent channels. (a) An unscoped pooled search on the
  critic's *own* paraphrase returned the gold at rank 1 in all four conditions simultaneously.
  (b) A whole-corpus non-embedding scan: `electrosp% AND liquid cryst%` returns 3 rows, which
  drops to exactly 1 once a sheath/core-shell condition is added. Therefore the ERC-COG + textiles
  predicate excluded zero competing satisfiers - the draft's discrimination table named theme-level
  neighbours that fail the *question* as well as the filter.
- **AMBIGUOUS-READING, MID.** "aiming to detect" has no object; the sentence admits both
  "which project" and "what does it detect", and the reference answered both.

Judge: both **UPHELD**, disposition **FIX**. Direction: keep the cell, keep the filter, keep the
gold, weaken the *text* until the predicate disambiguates; do not relabel to vector, because that
vacates the cell rather than filling it. Re-derive `term_style` rather than carrying it forward.

Orchestrator relay added one **sharpening, not a reversal**: if the weakened text still returns
the gold as a clean unscoped top-1, report that with ranks rather than weakening until the gold
becomes unretrievable.

**FRICTION EVENT #2** occurred here - see §7.2.

**Round 2.** The drafter's fix swapped the roles of the two clauses: the crowded functional
description ("wearable fibres for sensing hazardous gases and mechanical strain") now identifies,
and the corpus-unique mechanism (electrospinning LCs in a polymer sheath) is now the answer.
Measured effect, re-run on the exact new text:

|---|---|---|
| gold unscoped rank | 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 | 2 / 10 / 7 / 1 |
| unscoped top-1 | INTERACT (all four) | E-TEX (lexical, hybrid), Wearonics (dense), INTERACT (rerank) |

### hyb-11 - volcanology x IT - ACCEPTED (2 passes, 1 fix, candidate 1 of 3)

**Round 1.** Drafter returned an L3 survey with 8 gold of 15 survivors, having read all 15 and
pulled report sections for four borderline cases. That second read *flipped* ARISE2 from
"probably OUT" to IN. Three doubts recorded unprompted, including ARISE2's marginality.

Critic reported:

- **MISSED-GOLD, HIGH.** The question says "volcanology projects" but the gold was scoped on the
  euroSciVoc tag, which the question never states. Sweeping *outside* the tagged set found
  DeepCube (101004188, Italian participant TRE ALTAMIRA SRL, objective "UC4a: Automatic volcanic
  deformation detection and alerting", report "ground deformation patterns of volcanic unrest"),
  plus EUNADICS-AV and ALARM. The critic then grounded the finding in
  `src/retrieval/scoped.py` lines 57-67, whose whitelist excludes subject matter: **the runtime
  cannot build a topic filter**, so the scope it actually applies is `organization.country='IT'`,
  inside which all three live and are semantically retrievable.
- **GOLD-WRONG, MID.** ARISE2 characterises eruptions already in progress, not unrest or
  forecasting.

Judge: both **UPHELD**; disposition **FIX on MISSED-GOLD only**. It deliberately declined to make
GOLD-WRONG a target, sending it to the report as a recorded note, and explicitly rejected the
critic's alternative route (narrowing the topic) because that re-derives gold rather than
repairing scope.

**Round 2.** Drafter restated the scope as "projects classified under volcanology that have an
Italian participating organisation" (the hyb-03 precedent), recorded the three untagged satisfiers
and the `scoped.py` reason in `notes`, wrote ARISE2's marginality into `notes` per the ruling, and
voluntarily took a round-1 LOW by removing an inferred site attribution. It **disclosed** that it
had not re-run the pooled searches, since `pooling_evidence` had to stay byte-identical.

The orchestrator passed that disclosure to the fresh critic explicitly rather than letting it
slide. The critic **re-measured rather than trusting it**, and reproduced `pooled_candidate_count`
= 11 with all 8 gold still retrievable. It found:

- **unverifiable-scope-clause, MID** - and its suggested option (b) was *a revert of the wording
  the judge itself had ordered*. The orchestrator flagged that tension to the judge rather than
  leaving it to be inferred.
- **GOLD-WRONG, MID** - repeat class, argued on new grounds (the in/out line against excluded
  ENDGAME and PUSKURUM).
- Three LOW, including the `pooling_evidence` arithmetic blemish.

Judge: scope-clause **DISMISSED** (topic is never a structured filter, so the topical half of every
hybrid scope necessarily falls to retrieval; the revert would restore the round-1 HIGH);
GOLD-WRONG **UPHELD** and held at MID. Disposition **ACCEPT**, with an explicit stop-rule
interpretation - see §8.1.

### hyb-09 - the hard slot - ACCEPTED (4 passes, 2 candidates, 1 abandonment)

The only slot to reach a fallback, and the most informative.

**Candidate 1 (pinned): musicology x MSCA-IF.**

*Round 1.* The drafter **rejected the seed's own filter** at fit-check. Its reason: the bank brief
records this exact combination as the hyb-02 lesson ("a filter no user would state"), and the
scheme breakdown showed the bare code `MSCA-IF-EF-ST` would also have *split its own gold*
(STESICHOROS is `MSCA-IF-EF-RI`). It widened to `LIKE 'MSCA-IF%'` (46 survivors) and re-verified
from scratch. It also self-marked TERM-STYLE-HONEST as **WARN** rather than PASS.

Critic: REFERENCE-UNSUPPORTED **HIGH** (the reference's thesis "all three pair measurement with
digital re-creation" is false for MEMORISING - an eleven-term sweep over its full 8,045-char text
returned zero hits); REFERENCE-UNSUPPORTED **MID** (VRAASP's fieldwork sites misattributed);
FILTER-DECORATION **MID** (only the musicology conjunct discriminates).

Judge: HIGH and first MID **UPHELD**; FILTER-DECORATION **DISMISSED** on a substantive reading -
the brief's load-bearing test applies to the filter *as a whole*, and nowhere requires each
conjunct to discriminate. Disposition **FIX on REFERENCE-UNSUPPORTED only**, with an explicit
instruction not to touch the scheme half.

*Round 2.* The drafter rewrote the reference, dropping the three famous sites entirely rather than
re-attributing them. It also **drafted a `notes` addendum and then reverted it**, because the
direction said nothing outside `reference_answer` changes - and recorded that restraint in its
history rather than acting silently.

A fresh critic then found the defect that ended the candidate:

- **AMBIGUOUS-READING, HIGH.** It ran the blind-solve analogue - writing `filter_sql` itself from
  the question text plus schema docs *before* reading the drafter's - and produced a different
  filter. "Classified under musicology" reads naturally as `euroSciVocTitle = 'musicology'`,
  which schema_docs sd2 explicitly steers toward ("filter a whole area by path prefix, **a specific
  term by title**"). All three gold sit on sub-leaves (`musicology/ethnomusicology`,
  `musicology/popular music studies`) and none carries the `musicology` leaf. The narrow reading
  returns 23 survivors with **zero gold** and answers "none of them do this" against a 3-project
  gold. Two defensible readings, disjoint answers.
- REFERENCE-UNSUPPORTED, MID - on a clause the fix itself had introduced.

Judge: AMBIGUOUS-READING **UPHELD**; the MID **DISMISSED** (the critic conceded its own claim was
literally false, and upholding it would push the reference back into the misattribution upheld in
round 1). Disposition **ABANDON**: the defect is in the question text, the candidate's single fix
round was already spent, and "the cell would score a correctly-routed, schema_docs-faithful system
as having found nothing - a wrong finding in the study, not a degraded one."

Notably the candidate died **not on its data**. Gold, level, subtype and survivor set all survived
independent recomputation. It died on how its filter had to be *worded* against euroSciVoc.

**Candidate 2 (fallback): viticulture x SME-1.**

The orchestrator dispatched a fresh drafter with the abandoned candidate's **lesson** attached -
"check where your topic sits in the euroSciVoc path before wording the scope; word it so it has
exactly one executable reading" - but not its content. This is the bus relaying a known trap, not
carrying a verdict forward.

*Round 3.* **The lesson took.** The drafter ran an explicit one-reading check *before wording
anything*: `viticulture` is a leaf with no sub-terms and exactly one path, so the leaf and subtree
readings select the identical 46 projects. It also **rejected the seed's own framing** ("what
commercial innovations are pursued"), because all 14 survivors satisfy it - that is a survey, not a
synthesize - then considered and set aside two alternative sub-themes explicitly on *ambiguity*
grounds before choosing one with a doubly-qualified boundary.

Critic: no HIGH; one **MID, evidence-field-mismatch** - `pooling_evidence` mixes two denominators
(`pooled_candidate_count` 11 from the scoped pool, `rejected_count` 12 from the exhaustive
survivor read; 11 - 2 = 9). It independently reproduced the pool membership exactly and cited both
existing hybrid bank entries as holding the pool-consistent convention.

Judge: **UPHELD**, disposition **FIX**. Its reason for spending a pass on a provenance integer:
`bank.py` checks key presence and `accepted == gold_project_ids` and nothing else, so **no code path
will ever catch this**; it becomes permanent once promoted, and the correct value is determinate
rather than a judgement call.

*Round 4.* Fix applied (`rejected_count` 12 -> 9), and the exhaustive read survived in `notes`,
re-framed as **the authority for gold**, with the scoped pool demoted to diagnosing retrievability
only. The drafter declined both optional LOW fixes and put its reasoning on the record.

A fourth fresh critic escalated a point round 3 had rated LOW: it pulled Eternum's `workPerformed`
and found the SME-1 grant performed **no biological work at all** - only technical, financial and
commercial analysis - so the reference's present-indicative framing overstates the record.
**REFERENCE-UNSUPPORTED, MID.** It also confirmed the absence of the candidate-1 defect through a
channel nobody had used (all 14 survivors carry a phase-1 `subCall` code; all 6 SME-2 carry
phase-2 codes), and found an out-of-filter satisfier four previous nodes had missed
(vWISE 872394, "pied-de-cuve fermentation was used to preserve native microbiota").

Judge: **UPHELD**, held at MID, disposition **ACCEPT** - the fix round on this candidate was spent,
so the choice was accept-with-a-noted-MID or discard a sound instrument.

## 7. Friction log

### 7.1 Cold-start probe hang (setup)

First pooled `search_corpus` exceeded the 120 s tool timeout and had to be backgrounded, stopped
and retried. Cost ~7 min. **Suggested fix:** a cheap warm-up call (or a per-condition probe) before
the health probe, or a documented expectation that the first pooled call after server start is slow.

### 7.2 Three agents killed mid-flight by `ENOTFOUND`

The hyb-08 fix drafter, the hyb-09 critic and the hyb-11 critic all died simultaneously on
`API Error: Unable to connect to API (ENOTFOUND)` - a transport failure, not a servers-down signal,
so the outage path did not apply.

**What worked:** resuming each from its transcript via `SendMessage` with a short "you were cut off,
carry on from X" note. All three resumed exactly where they stopped and completed normally; the
hyb-08 drafter had already said "both sides re-measured, running the precheck gate" and picked up
there. No candidate was consumed and no work was lost.

**Gap in the skill:** the bound is written as "retry that agent once", which implies a respawn.
**Resume-from-transcript is strictly better** and should be written into the skill as the preferred
form of the retry, with respawn as the fallback when the transcript is unusable.

### 7.3 Journal assembly is the orchestrator's dominant cost

Every transition required hand-writing a Python script to append a journal line carrying `record`,
`evidence`, `checklist`, `why_good`, `findings`, `judge_decisions` and `history` verbatim. Eighteen
such scripts were written (`j2.py` .. `j18.py`, plus three patch scripts). This is slow, repetitive
and the single largest orchestrator burden.

**Suggested fix:** a deterministic `journal-append` CLI command taking the node's returned package
plus a status, doing the envelope construction and latest-line-wins bookkeeping in code. The
orchestrator should be relaying, not marshalling JSON.

### 7.4 HTML entity corruption in returned packages - HIGHEST-PRIORITY FIX

Agent-returned packages contained `&lt;`, `&gt;` and `&amp;` where the source text had `<`, `>` and
`&` (e.g. `S=7 &lt;= 20`, `fruits &amp; vegetables`, `-&gt;`). These were unescaped by hand before
staging each record.

**Unnoticed, corrupted text would have gone into the bank.** This is the only failure mode observed
in the run that is both silent and permanent. It exists because the drafter has no shell, so the
orchestrator must transcribe the returned record to disk before `validate-record` can see it - and
that transcription step is entirely unguarded.

**Suggested fixes, in order of preference:** (a) have the drafter write its record through a
deterministic path rather than returning it as prose to be re-typed; (b) add an entity-detection
check to `validate-record` that fails a record containing `&lt;` / `&gt;` / `&amp;` / `&quot;`;
(c) at minimum, document the hazard in the skill.

### 7.5 `pooling_evidence` has an unenforced convention

Every existing bank entry holds `pooled_candidate_count = |accepted| + rejected_count`. `bank.py`
checks key presence and `accepted == gold_project_ids`, and never the arithmetic. Two independent
critics on two different slots found violations unprompted, and **the two judges priced them
differently** - LOW and a note on hyb-11, a spent drafter pass on hyb-09.

**Suggested fix:** add the arithmetic check to `validate-record`. It costs nothing, never varies,
and would have saved a full drafter pass in this run.

### 7.6 Critics re-attack everything after a fix

Even a two-field provenance edit triggered a full fresh attack (hyb-09 round 4: 76,868 tokens,
14 tool calls). Expensive - but that round is exactly where the Eternum modality defect was caught
and escalated from LOW to MID. **Recommendation: keep the behaviour, but state the cost in the
skill** so it is a chosen trade rather than a surprise.

### 7.7 The drafter cannot run `validate-record`

Noted by the hyb-11 drafter itself: "I have no shell in this role, so `python -m src.cli
validate-record` is the orchestrator's step." It compensated by checking conformance against
`bank.py` by inspection. This is the same root cause as 7.4 and would be closed by the same fix.

### 7.8 Thin paraphrase seed supply

The corpus profile holds 10 hybrid seeds, only **2** of them `paraphrase`. Slot hyb-09's fallback
list was therefore forced onto near-duplicate topics (both viticulture). When the pinned candidate
was abandoned, the slot had exactly one good fallback. **`/explore-corpus` should be asked for
paraphrase-friendly hybrid seeds specifically.**

## 8. Design questions this run raised

### 8.1 The within-candidate stop rule's trigger and intent come apart

The rule fires when "the same defect class is upheld twice on one candidate", and its purpose is to
detect *a fix that did not take*. hyb-11's judge upheld GOLD-WRONG twice and **declined to fire it**,
reasoning explicitly:

> "its trigger means 'the fix did not take', and no fix round was ever spent on this class - I
> deliberately declined to target it in round 1 and sent it to the report as a note, so the second
> UPHELD is a fresh critic re-finding that same recorded note, not a failed repair; reading it
> literally here would make 'let the MID ride into the report' impossible in practice."

That is sound, and it was made explicitly rather than silently - the design working. But it means
the rule as written does not distinguish *repeat-because-unfixed-by-choice* from
*repeat-because-the-repair-failed*, and each judge will have to re-derive that distinction.
**Recommendation: rewrite the trigger in terms of "upheld again after a fix round targeting that
class".**

### 8.2 Identical defects get different prices across slots

Judges see only their own slot, by design. The `pooling_evidence` break was consequently priced two
ways in one batch. The isolation is correct; the variance is the cost. **The remedy is not to break
isolation but to move determinable checks into code** (§7.5).

### 8.3 Marginal-gold findings have no home

hyb-11's ARISE2 was upheld twice as a marginal member and rode into the report both times, because
fixing it would re-derive gold. That is the right outcome, but it means the judge must keep
re-ruling on a known, accepted note every round. **A "recorded and accepted" state on a finding
would stop later critics' re-discoveries from consuming judge attention.**

### 8.4 What the orchestrator relays after an abandonment

When hyb-09 advanced to its fallback, the orchestrator passed the abandoned candidate's *lesson*
("check the euroSciVoc path shape before wording the scope") without its content. The next drafter
acted on it immediately and the class of defect did not recur - it ran the one-reading check as its
second action. This is not currently in the skill. **Recommendation: make "relay the lesson, never
the verdict or the content" an explicit orchestrator duty on candidate advance.**

### 8.5 Carried-forward evidence needs a protocol

hyb-11's drafter did not re-run pooled searches after a text edit because `pooling_evidence` had to
stay byte-identical, and disclosed it. The orchestrator passed the disclosure to the critic, which
re-measured and confirmed. That worked, but only because the disclosure was voluntary.
**Recommendation: require an explicit `evidence_carried_forward` flag on any fix round that does not
re-run measurements, and require the orchestrator to pass it to the critic.**

## 9. What the split of authority actually bought

Concrete instances where the separation changed an outcome:

1. **The critic found what the drafter's own gate could not.** `precheck_record` passed on the first
   call for all four slots. Every HIGH finding came from a node that did not author the record.
2. **The drafter self-reported doubts that later became findings.** hyb-08's unscoped rank-1 doubt
   and hyb-11's ARISE2 marginality were both volunteered before any critic saw them. A node that
   also adjudicated its own quality would have had an incentive to suppress those.
3. **The judge dismissed 3 of 8 MIDs on substantive grounds** - including one whose suggested fix
   would have reverted the judge's own prior order. A reviewer-rules pipeline has no node positioned
   to say "that finding is real but not a defect under the standard".
4. **The judge overrode a critic's severity in both directions** - holding GOLD-WRONG at MID rather
   than escalating, and spending a pass on a MID provenance field another judge let ride.
5. **The bus never adjudicated.** Where the orchestrator had views (the revert tension, the
   cross-slot convention issue, the budget constraints), it labelled them as context and passed them
   to the judge rather than acting.
6. **Fresh-critic-per-round earned its cost twice.** hyb-08's round-2 critic argued *against* the
   round-1 finding on its own evidence; hyb-09's round-4 critic escalated what round 3 had called
   LOW. A warm critic would likely have defended its earlier position in the first case and skipped
   the re-read in the second.
